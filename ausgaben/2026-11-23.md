## 1. Leverage points in systems
**Donella Meadows ranks twelve places to intervene in a system and argues that the obvious ones (numbers, subsidies, taxes) are the weakest, while shared mindsets sit near the top.**

Field: Thinking · Surfaced in: [Hacker News search results on systems thinking (no single thread)](https://news.ycombinator.com/) · Source (essay): [Meadows, 1999, Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/)

Criteria:
- "Leverage points are points of power." (intro)
- "Counterintuitive. That's Forrester's word to describe complex systems." (intro)
- "Constants, parameters, numbers (such as subsidies, taxes, standards)." (Places to intervene, item 12 (lowest))
- "The mindset or paradigm out of which the system — its goals, structure, rules, delays, parameters — arises." (Places to intervene, item 2)
- "The structure of information flows (who does and does not have access to information)." (Places to intervene, item 6)

Related: Stocks and flows, Forrester's system dynamics
Topic: `leverage-points-meadows`

## 2. CRDT (conflict-free replicated data type)
**A data structure that many devices can edit offline and concurrently, and that merges automatically so all copies end up identical without a central server deciding.**

Field: Software · Surfaced in: [Hacker News threads on CRDTs and local-first software](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37584049) · Source (paper): [Kleppmann & Beresford, 2017, A Conflict-Free Replicated JSON Datatype](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.03960)

Criteria:
- "All replicas of the data structure should automatically converge towards the same state (a requirement known as strong eventual consistency [3])." (section 1, design goals)
- "No user input should be lost due to concurrent modifications." (section 1, design goals)
- "If all sequential permutations of a set of updates lead to the same state, then concurrent execution of those updates also leads to the same state [4]." (section 1, design goals)
- "The algorithm performs all merging client-side and does not depend on ordering guarantees from the network" (Abstract)

Related: Strong eventual consistency, local-first software
Topic: `crdt`

## 3. Constitutional AI
**Instead of humans labelling harmful outputs, a model critiques and revises its own answers against a written list of principles, and AI preference judgments replace human ones in reinforcement learning.**

Field: AI · Surfaced in: [Hacker News search results on AI alignment methods](https://news.ycombinator.com/) · Source (paper): [Bai et al., 2022, Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073)

Criteria:
- "As AI systems become more capable, we would like to enlist their help to supervise other AIs." (Abstract)
- "The only human oversight is provided through a list of rules or principles" (Abstract)
- "In the supervised phase we sample from an initial model, then generate self-critiques and revisions, and then finetune the original model on revised responses." (Abstract)
- "we use ‘RL from AI Feedback’ (RLAIF)" (Abstract)

Related: RLHF, preference models
Topic: `constitutional-ai`
